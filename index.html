Where the cards actually come from

  • Privacy.com issues the numbers; this page only tracks them. An app can't mint real card numbers without a licensed issuer behind it.
  • Capital One gives free virtual numbers per merchant on any of its open cards.
  • Apple Card has its own virtual number in Wallet, and Klarna makes one-time cards.
  • Log label, last 4, merchant and limit here. Never the full number, expiry or CVV.
